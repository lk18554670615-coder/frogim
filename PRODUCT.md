# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who use an instant-messaging app throughout the day on iPhone, Android, tablet, desktop, and the web. They need to find conversations, read status at a glance, send messages quickly, and understand privacy and moderation actions without learning a new interaction language.

## Product Purpose

青蛙呱呱提供可靠的单聊和群聊、清晰的消息状态、可恢复同步、实用群管理与可见安全工具。成功意味着沟通即时、导航稳定，并且用户能够理解每条消息和账号操作发生了什么。

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, premium. The product speaks plainly and behaves with restraint. It should feel native to an Apple platform while preserving a distinct, neutral identity that also works on Android and the web.

## Anti-references

- The initial warm-white and teal concept is an explicit anti-reference: oversized headings, thick rounded buttons, decorative cards, Android-style icon weight, excessive empty space, and high-chroma brand color across ordinary controls.
- Do not imitate WeChat layouts, assets, naming, or metadata.
- Avoid generic AI-app aesthetics: cream surfaces, giant slogans, decorative gradients, excessive blur, glass content cards, 24px-plus card radii, and animation without state meaning.

## Design Principles

1. Content leads; controls stay visually quiet until needed.
2. Use familiar platform navigation and action placement before inventing a pattern.
3. Communicate reliability through explicit state, predictable recovery, and immediate feedback.
4. Reserve brand emphasis for selection, primary action, and meaningful status.
5. Keep every interaction usable with larger text, reduced motion, high contrast, and assistive technology.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for shared interfaces and follow Apple accessibility guidance on iOS. Support Dynamic Type-equivalent scaling, at least 44 by 44 point targets, VoiceOver labels, reduced motion, increased contrast, reduced transparency, color-independent status, and layouts that remain usable at 200 percent text scaling where practical.
